# Prompts for icons and UI art (one image per sheet)

Give each prompt to an image AI, then send the single image back so it can be cropped. Rules every sheet follows: one image, equal square cells in a visible grid order, the same flat near-black background (#05070C) in every cell, NO text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere, every symbol centred with equal margin, same stroke weight and glow across the sheet, 4096 px wide if possible.

## Sheet 1: icons, blue glass (6 columns x 4 rows, 24 cells)

Order, left to right then down: home, back arrow, phone, person, microphone, music note, speaker mute, radio, gear, navigation arrow, map pin, video play-button, play, pause, previous, next, 360-degree car from above, apps grid (3x3 dots), sun, moon, end-call, tyre with pressure mark, speedometer, clock.

Prompt: "One single image: a 6 by 4 grid of 24 equal square cells, flat near-black background (#05070C), thin dark grid lines. Each cell holds ONE clean line icon, centred, same size, in the order: home, back arrow, phone, person, microphone, music note, speaker mute, radio, gear, navigation arrow, map pin, video play button, play triangle, pause, previous track, next track, 360-degree car from above, apps grid of nine dots, sun, moon, end call, tyre with pressure mark, speedometer, clock. Style: premium car-infotainment liquid glass, soft cyan-blue glow, thin bright rim light, subtle inner gradient, rounded line ends, identical stroke weight on every icon. No text, no numbers, no logos, no shadows outside each cell."

## Sheet 2: icons, red glass (same 24 cells, same order)

Prompt: use the Sheet 1 prompt and replace "soft cyan-blue glow" with "soft red glow". (Used for the primary contact and warnings.)

## Sheet 3: UI pieces (4 columns x 3 rows, wide cells)

Cells: (1) rounded glass contacts bar, empty, (2) dark plain dock bar, empty, (3) status-bar slab left, (4) status-bar slab right, (5) glass info box (city), (6) glass info box (navigation), (7) round glass button, normal, (8) round glass button, pressed, (9) round glass button, red, (10) 360 camera window frame (square corners, empty), (11) black screen corner piece with rounded inner corner, (12) tab selector chip, active and inactive side by side.

Prompt: "One single image: a 4 by 3 grid of 12 equal wide cells on flat near-black (#05070C). Each cell shows one empty interface element for a luxury car infotainment screen, centred, in this order: [list above]. Style: dark navy liquid glass, thin bright rim light, soft blue glow, smooth rounded shapes, no content inside the elements. No text, numbers or logos."

## Sheet 4: whole screens (2 columns x 3 rows, 16:9 cells)

Cells: (1) simple layout, driver on the right, (2) the same mirrored for driver on the left, (3) layout with the 360 window turned off, (4) extended layout with bottom bar, (5) day theme, (6) speed warning, red glow along the top.

Prompt: "One single image: a 2 by 3 grid of six 16:9 car infotainment screens. Dark navy glass style, line-art offline map with soft vignette, one rounded glass contacts bar, plain black dock at the bottom, small 360-degree camera window at one side, glass info boxes on the other side. Cells: simple layout (camera window on the right); same mirrored (window on the left); no camera window; extended bottom bar; light day theme; speed warning with a red glow along the top third. No readable text, no logos, no brand names."

## Sheet 5: app icons (3 columns x 1 row)

Prompt: "One single image: three rounded-square app icons side by side on near-black: (1) installer: a house with a downward arrow, (2) launcher: a house inside a glowing ring, (3) call plugin: a phone handset with a small microphone. Dark navy glass, blue glow. No text, no logos."
