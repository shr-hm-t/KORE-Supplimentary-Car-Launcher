# Prompts for showcase images (for the README)

These make pictures that look like the launcher on a car screen. They are artist's impressions made with an image AI, not screenshots: say so wherever you use them. Real photos of your own unit are better when you have them. Every prompt forbids logos and brand names.

Send the single result back and it can be cropped into the four pictures.

## Four pictures in one image (2 x 2)

One single landscape image (3:2) divided by thin dark lines into a 2 by 2 grid of four equal photographs. No text, letters, numbers, logos or brand names anywhere, except none at all on the screens. Each cell shows the same wide car infotainment screen (dark navy glass, thin light-silver line icons, flat, no glow) mounted in a dark car dashboard at a slight angle, soft studio lighting, shallow depth of field, premium look.

Cell 1 (top left): Home screen. A dark line-art offline road map fills the screen with a soft darkening along the edges. At the bottom, one wide rounded glass bar with five equal sections separated by thin vertical lines: a radio icon, a 360-degree car icon, a map-pin icon, and two phone icons. A small round Home button sits at the left edge. A small badge logo (no real brand) sits in the top left corner. Right-aligned turn arrow and street name in white at the top right, no boxes behind them.

Cell 2 (top right): The same screen with a small square 360-degree camera view (top-down car with four camera angles) on the right side and the map on the left. The bottom bar has the rounded glass contact bar and a plain black bar of small white icons below it.

Cell 3 (bottom left): The same map screen at speed with a thin red glow along the top third of the screen, a speed number is not shown. Calm and clean.

Cell 4 (bottom right): The large app grid: a 6 by 3 grid of generic rounded app icons (no real app logos) on dark glass, labels as blurred shapes.

## Single hero image

A wide premium photograph of a modern compact SUV dashboard at dusk with the car infotainment screen showing a dark line-art offline map, one rounded glass contact bar with thin dividers at the bottom, a small camera view on the right, thin light-silver flat icons. No logos, no brand names, no readable text. Soft reflections on the glass, shallow depth of field.

## Start-up screen

A wide car screen showing a dark navy background with one large soft glowing light in the middle, warm amber blending into cool blue, and a small round badge in the centre. Minimal, calm, no text, no logos.
