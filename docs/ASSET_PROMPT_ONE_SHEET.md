# One-sheet prompt for icons, UI pieces, screens and app icons

Paste the prompt below into your image AI. Ask for the largest size it can make (landscape 3:2). Send the single result back and I will crop it.

If the AI makes the symbols too small or muddled, use the five separate prompts in ASSET_PROMPTS.md instead; a single sheet this crowded is harder for image AIs.

## Prompt

One single landscape image (3:2), a strict production sheet on a flat near-black background (#05070C), divided by thin dark grid lines into four horizontal bands from top to bottom. No text, letters, numbers, logos or brand names anywhere. Every item is centred in its own cell with equal margin. Style everywhere: premium car-infotainment liquid glass, dark navy, thin bright rim light, soft glow, rounded line ends, identical stroke weight.

BAND 1 (top, blue icons): a grid of 12 columns by 2 rows, 24 equal square cells, each with one clean line icon with a soft cyan-blue glow. Order left to right, then next row: home, back arrow, phone, person, microphone, music note, speaker mute, radio, gear, navigation arrow, map pin, video play button; play triangle, pause, previous track, next track, 360-degree car seen from above, apps grid of nine dots, sun, moon, end call, tyre with pressure mark, speedometer, clock.

BAND 2 (red icons): exactly the same 24 icons in the same order and grid (12 columns by 2 rows), with a soft red glow instead of blue.

BAND 3 (UI pieces, empty, no content inside): a grid of 6 columns by 2 rows, 12 equal wide cells. Order: rounded glass contacts bar; plain dark dock bar; status-bar slab left; status-bar slab right; glass info box (city); glass info box (navigation); round glass button normal; round glass button pressed; round glass button red; square-cornered 360 camera window frame; black screen corner piece with rounded inner corner; two tab chips side by side (active and inactive).

BAND 4 (bottom): on the left, 3 rounded-square app icons side by side (installer: house with a downward arrow; launcher: house inside a glowing ring; call plugin: phone handset with a small microphone). On the right, a grid of 3 columns by 2 rows of six 16:9 car screens: simple layout with a small 360 camera window on the right; the same mirrored with the window on the left; no camera window; extended bottom bar; light day theme; speed warning with a red glow along the top third. Screens use a line-art offline map with soft vignette, one rounded glass contacts bar, a plain black dock, glass info boxes, and no readable text.
